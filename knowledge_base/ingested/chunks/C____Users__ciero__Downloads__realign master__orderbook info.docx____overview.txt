Order-Book Trading Algorithms
In this chapter, we venture into the world of Algorithmic Trading and specifically, we cover a couple of problems involving a trading Order Book that can be cast as Markov Decision Processes, and hence tackled with Dynamic Programming or Reinforcement Learning. We start the chapter by covering the basics of how trade orders are submitted and executed on an Order Book, a structure that allows for eﬀicient transactions between buyers and sellers of a financial asset. Without loss of generality, we refer to the financial asset being traded on the Order Book as a “stock” and the number of units of the asset as “shares.” Next we will explain how a large trade can significantly shift the Order Book, a phenomenon known as Price Impact.
