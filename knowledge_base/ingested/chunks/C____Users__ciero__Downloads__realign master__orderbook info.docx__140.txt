Avellaneda-Stoikov Continuous-Time Formulation
A landmark paper by Avellaneda and Stoikov (Avellaneda and Stoikov 2008) formulated this optimal market-making problem in it’s continuous-time version. Their formulation is conducive to analytical tractability and they came up with a simple, clean and intuitive solution.
