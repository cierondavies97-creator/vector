Dynamics. We shall refer to the OB activity of an MO immediately “eating into the Buy LOs” (and hence, potentially transacting at prices lower than the best price) as the Tempo-rary Price Impact. As mentioned earlier, this is followed by subsequent replenishment of both Buy and Sell LOs on the OB (stabilizing the OB) - we refer to any eventual (end of the hour) lowering of the Best Bid Price (relative to the Best Bid Price before the MO was submitted) as the Permanent Price Impact. Modeling the temporary and permanent Price Impacts separately helps us in deciding on the optimal actions (optimal shares to be sold at the start of each hour).
