Note that we have intentionally not specified if Pt, Rt, Nt are integers or real numbers, or if constrained to be non-negative etc. Those precise specifications will be customized to the nuances/constraints of the specific Optimal Order Execution problem we’d be solving. Be default, we shall assume that Pt ∈ R+ and Nt, Rt ∈ Z≥0 (as these represent realistic trading situations), although we do consider special cases later in the chapter where Pt, Rt ∈ R (unconstrained real numbers for analytical tractability).
